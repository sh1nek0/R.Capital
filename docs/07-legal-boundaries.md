# Legal and Product Boundaries

## Product Positioning

Capital OS Navigator is an informational diagnostic SaaS. It helps founders prepare for fundraising and understand possible routes.

## Mandatory Disclaimer

Capital OS Navigator is not an investment platform, does not accept investor money, does not organize investment agreements, and does not guarantee financing. Recommendations are informational and preparatory.

## MVP Must Not Include

- investor onboarding;
- investment account;
- money transfer;
- public investment offer;
- equity marketplace;
- transaction execution;
- promised yield;
- partner transfer without consent.

## Personal Data Requirements

Production release must include:

- privacy policy;
- personal data processing consent;
- consent versioning;
- storage in the required jurisdictional contour;
- data deletion process;
- admin access restrictions;
- audit logging.
