# Questionnaire Structure

## Section 1. Contacts

- founder name;
- email;
- telegram;
- phone;
- consent checkbox.

## Section 2. Project

- startup name;
- description;
- website;
- industry;
- city;
- client type;
- business model.

## Section 3. Stage

- idea;
- prototype;
- MVP;
- traction;
- revenue;
- growth.

## Section 4. Traction

- users;
- pilots;
- paying customers;
- revenue range;
- growth signals;
- partnerships.

## Section 5. Funding Need

- amount;
- purpose;
- preferred funding types;
- previous attempts;
- urgency.

## Section 6. Documents

- pitch deck;
- one-pager;
- financial model;
- cap table;
- legal entity;
- IP documents;
- customer proof;
- data room.

## Section 7. Pain and Intent

- main pain;
- interest in interview;
- readiness to pay for expert review;
- preferred contact format.
